# okf-drone-skill DRN-10 — PX4 SITL flight check

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** draft; prototyped in the agent container (PX4 v1.17.0 SITL, pymavlink 2.4.50)
- **Backlog:** DRN-10
- **Base:** `docs/specs/2026-09-28-okf-drone-skill-v1-design.md` (v1). This spec adds an optional
  step; it changes no v1 contract.

## 1. Goal and definition of done

Fly a generated `mission.plan` in PX4 software-in-the-loop (SITL) and check the flown track
against the plan and the bundle. The flight is more evidence for the v1 rules. It is not an
approval, and it does not change the decision that `render_report` proposes.

**Done when:**

1. `make px4` builds the pinned PX4 SITL in `.tools/px4/` (not committed).
2. `make sitl MISSION=missions/<id>.yaml` (after `make plan`) starts PX4, flies the plan, stops
   PX4, and writes `out/<id>/sitl.json`, `out/<id>/sitl_track.json` and `out/<id>/sitl/`
   (the PX4 log `px4.log` and the flight log `.ulg`).
3. Each result in `sitl.json` cites the verified concept that governs its check id, or is a gap.
4. The offline tests pass on recorded tracks from real SITL flights, without PX4.
5. The owner flies m01 to m05 on the owner's machine and reads the results (human gate).

**Out of scope:** Gazebo and 3D views, fixed-wing and VTOL, wind and sensor faults, failsafe
injection (for example a real data-link loss), the report section for SITL, and CI runs.

## 2. Guardrails

- **Optional.** `make plan`, `make verify`, `make seeded` and `make examples` do not need PX4
  or pymavlink. pymavlink is imported only when the flight starts.
- **Same rules, same concepts.** The flight checks reuse the v1 check ids and read their values
  from the same `table:` entries. No threshold in code.
- **No LLM.**
- **Evidence, not approval.** A SITL pass is not a GO. A SITL fail is shown to the person who
  signs; v1 does not merge it into the decision.

## 3. Pinned versions

| Dependency | Pin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PX4 | `PX4_VERSION=v1.17.0` in `tools.lock` | Same version as the S7 parameter reference. Clone with submodules, `make px4_sitl_default`. |
| Simulator | SIH quadrotor, airframe `10040_sihsim_quadx` | PX4's built-in simulator: headless, no Gazebo. |
| pymavlink | `pymavlink==2.4.50`, extra `sitl` in `pyproject.toml` | Pure Python, thus no platform wheel. `mavlink_gcs.py` is the small ground station on it. |

## 4. Data contracts

### 4.1 `sitl_track.json`

A list of samples, one per `0.2 s` of wall time:
`{t_s, lat, lon, rel_alt_m, armed, in_air, mission_seq, mission_total}`.
`mission_seq` is the mission progress from MAVLink `MISSION_CURRENT`: it equals `mission_total`
when `mission_state` is complete.

### 4.2 `sitl.json`

`{mission_id, px4_version, simulator, summary, checks}`.

- `summary`: `track_points`, `sim_flight_time_s` (wall time x speed factor), `max_rel_alt_m`,
  `params_read_back` (the failsafe parameters as PX4 reports them), and `px4_warnings` (the
  `WARN` lines of `px4.log`, in order, without repeats). The warnings show why PX4 refused a
  mission, for example "Geofence violation for waypoint 3".
- `checks`: results in the v1 form (spec §5.3):

| Check id | Concept | Flight evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `alt.max_agl` | `regulations/easa-open` | highest airborne `rel_alt_m` against `max_height_above_surface_m`; same applicability as v1 (open category, flat terrain) |
| `plan.inside_geofence` | `failsafes/geofence-breach` | every airborne sample inside an inclusion polygon of the plan |
| `plan.last_item_return` | `mavlink/nav-rtl` | mission progress complete, and the last sample landed and disarmed |
| `failsafe.lost_link` | `failsafes/lost-link` | `NAV_DLL_ACT` read back equals `px4_action.values[declared action]` |
| `failsafe.geofence_breach` | `failsafes/geofence-breach` | `GF_ACTION` read back equals `px4_action.values[declared action]` |

- No airborne sample (PX4 refused the mission, or the vehicle did not take off): the altitude
  and geofence checks are `gap` ("not flown"), and `plan.last_item_return` fails.

## 5. Flight procedure

1. Start PX4 with `PX4_SYS_AUTOSTART=10040`, `PX4_HOME_LAT/LON/ALT` from the mission `home`,
   `PX4_SIM_SPEED_FACTOR` (default 10), `HEADLESS=1`, working folder `out/<id>/sitl/`.
2. Connect as a ground station on `udpin://0.0.0.0:14550` (the PX4 "Normal" link). PX4 counts
   a GCS only on that link; with `NAV_DLL_ACT` set, arming needs a GCS.
3. Wait for `health_all_ok`. Set the failsafe parameters from `px4_action` of the declared
   actions, and read them back.
4. Import the plan with `import_qgroundcontrol_mission_from_string`; upload the geofence, then
   the mission.
5. Arm (retry while PX4 registers the GCS). Start the mission (retry while PX4 checks the new
   mission). Sample until the vehicle has flown and disarmed, or PX4 disarms without a flight,
   or the timeout.
6. Stop PX4. Check the track. Write the files.

## 6. Testing

- Offline: recorded tracks from the prototype flights (m01 full flight, m02 above the limit,
  m03 refused by PX4) are test fixtures for `sitl_check`. The parameter mapping and the PX4
  environment are unit-tested.
- With PX4: the owner runs `make px4` once, then `make sitl` for m01 to m05 (human gate). The
  expected results are in the plan.

## 7. Decisions (proposed; the owner confirms them with the plan)

1. SITL is optional and outside `make plan`. Its results are evidence for the person who signs;
   they do not change the proposed decision.
2. The flight checks reuse the v1 check ids and concepts. No new concept, no new value.
3. PX4 `v1.17.0` (the S7 version), the SIH quadrotor, and pymavlink `2.4.50`; the pins are
   exact. The first prototype used MAVSDK `4.0.1`. Its aarch64 Linux wheel does not work: the
   library does not declare libstdc++ as a dependency, and with libstdc++ loaded first it stops
   with a segmentation fault (exit 139). pymavlink replaced it; the m01 to m05 statuses did not
   change.
4. No flight means no flight evidence: the altitude and geofence checks are gaps, and the
   return check fails.
5. `sitl.json` keeps PX4's `WARN` lines, so a refusal by PX4 shows its reason.
6. The test fixtures are tracks recorded in real SITL flights of m01, m02 and m03 (about
   106 KB in total). They are data, not typed values.
7. Human gate: the owner flies m01 to m05 on the owner's machine and compares the results with
   the plan.
